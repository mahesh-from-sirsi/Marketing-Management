Marketing Assignments

12 team-friendly assignments across five modules. Use fictional data when field research is not possible. Students should submit their own analysis and disclose AI assistance.

Standard submission and rubric

Submit a decision memo (400–700 words), supporting tables/calculations, evidence or explicit assumptions, and a 100-word reflection. Teams of 3–5 may divide research, analysis, creative work and presentation.

Marks /100: concept accuracy 25, evidence and assumptions 20, analytical reasoning 25, feasibility 15, communication 10, ethics 5.

Module 1 • Assignment 01

Need Detective

Scenario: Interview five consenting adults about a recent low-cost purchase. Separate stated need, real need, want and demand.

Deliverables: Anonymised insight table, two interview quotes with permission, and a 250-word interpretation.

Quality check: Do not record names, phone numbers or sensitive data.

Module 1 • Assignment 02

₹30 Snack Launch

Scenario: Design a four-P launch for a hypothetical millet snack targeting commuters or students.

Deliverables: One-page product proposition, price rationale, two channels, promotional message and success metrics.

Quality check: State costs as assumptions, not verified market prices.

Module 1 • Assignment 03

Marketing or Selling?

Scenario: Evaluate five fictional promotional messages and distinguish customer-oriented marketing from transaction-focused selling.

Deliverables: Classification matrix with reasons and rewritten customer-value messages.

Quality check: Avoid unsupported claims about real companies.

Module 1 • Assignment 04

Environment Scan

Scenario: Assess a fictional Shimoga café considering online orders using PESTLE and SWOT.

Deliverables: PESTLE table, SWOT matrix, three decisions and evidence gaps.

Quality check: Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions.

Module 2 • Assignment 05

Segment a Local Market

Scenario: Segment potential customers for a budget fitness centre by needs and observable behaviour.

Deliverables: Three segments, segment attractiveness matrix and a target recommendation.

Quality check: Avoid stereotyping protected groups.

Module 2 • Assignment 06

Black Box Buying Journey

Scenario: Analyse the purchase journey of a fictional first-time laptop buyer.

Deliverables: Stimulus–black box–response diagram and five-stage buying journey.

Quality check: Identify where information is hypothetical.

Module 3 • Assignment 07

Product Life Cycle Decision

Scenario: A fictional packaged juice brand has slowing growth. Recommend product-mix and life-cycle actions.

Deliverables: Two options, risk assessment and measurable launch test.

Quality check: Do not claim stage is proven without sales evidence.

Module 3 • Assignment 08

Pricing Challenge

Scenario: A snack costs ₹18 variable cost per unit with ₹24,000 fixed cost for a campaign. Compare ₹30 and ₹35 prices at different volumes.

Deliverables: Contribution calculations, break-even units, sensitivity table and recommendation.

Quality check: Show assumptions and formula.

Module 4 • Assignment 09

Channel Conflict Boardroom

Scenario: A manufacturer discounts online while local retailers complain about undercutting.

Deliverables: Stakeholder map, two channel policies, trade-offs and proposed resolution.

Quality check: Consider legal and contractual uncertainty.

Module 4 • Assignment 10

AIDA Campaign Audit

Scenario: Audit two fictional ads for attention, interest, desire and action.

Deliverables: AIDA matrix, revised copy and a small A/B testing plan.

Quality check: Avoid fabricated performance claims.

Module 5 • Assignment 11

Competitive Strategy Sprint

Scenario: A startup enters a market with a strong leader and several followers.

Deliverables: Competitor map, target niche, positioning and 90-day plan.

Quality check: Separate evidence from assumptions.

Module 5 • Assignment 12

Ethical Digital Marketing

Scenario: A fictional influencer promotion lacks sponsorship disclosure and exaggerates sustainability claims.

Deliverables: Risk assessment, corrected copy and a responsible measurement plan.

Quality check: No deceptive advertising or personal-data collection.
