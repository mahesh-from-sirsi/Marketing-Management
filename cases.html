Marketing Case Studies

Five original fictional cases. They are not presented as verified histories of real companies.

Module 1

The Millet Snack Launch

A fictional college-area retailer notices high afternoon demand but slow sales of healthy snacks. A supplier offers a millet product at a quoted wholesale price, but students question taste and value.

Questions for the team

Identify needs versus wants; propose a value proposition and 4Ps; state what to test before launch.

Expected output: 5-minute presentation, one decision, two alternatives, evidence gaps, measurable next step.

Module 2

The Wrong Customer

A gym advertises only to young professionals but its enquiries increasingly come from older first-time customers and students.

Questions for the team

Create evidence-based segments, select a target, and draft positioning without stereotyping.

Expected output: 5-minute presentation, one decision, two alternatives, evidence gaps, measurable next step.

Module 3

Brand Extension Dilemma

A fictional tea brand known for affordability plans to launch a premium herbal line under the same name.

Questions for the team

Assess extension benefits and dilution risks; propose packaging and a testable pricing approach.

Expected output: 5-minute presentation, one decision, two alternatives, evidence gaps, measurable next step.

Module 4

Online vs Retail

A local home-appliance company offers online coupons unavailable to its retail partners. Retailers threaten to reduce shelf space.

Questions for the team

Diagnose channel conflict, propose fair alternatives, and create a communication plan.

Expected output: 5-minute presentation, one decision, two alternatives, evidence gaps, measurable next step.

Module 5

Green Claim Under Scrutiny

A fictional apparel startup promotes its range as '100% eco-friendly' without independent evidence.

Questions for the team

Assess ethical and strategic risks, rewrite the claim and propose an audit checklist.

Expected output: 5-minute presentation, one decision, two alternatives, evidence gaps, measurable next step.
