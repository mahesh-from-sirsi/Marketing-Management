## Editorial status

Module 1 contains continuous teaching notes across its syllabus topics, with examples, comparison tables and revision questions. Modules 2–5 **do not yet contain full explanatory notes**; they currently provide the prescribed syllabus and a topic-wise checklist. The assignment bank and MCQ practice are starter resources and are not represented as verified VTU previous-year questions.

All business scenarios are fictional unless explicitly stated otherwise. The syllabus follows the faculty-provided text; Module 2's repeated phrase is retained in the syllabus but consolidated in the checklist.
